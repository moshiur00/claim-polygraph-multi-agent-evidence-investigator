# Phase 8 Stage 8.7 completion report

Date: 28 July 2026

Status: Complete

## Outcome

The promoted LangGraph path now runs independent defender and challenger
position builders over the authoritative approved-evidence packet, joins their
typed outputs and deterministically reconciles them through the existing
`build_argument_ledger` policy.

The workflow demonstrates bounded adversarial multi-agent behavior without a
free-form debate. The roles cannot search, fetch, approve evidence, create
facts or change a verdict.

## Role isolation

Both roles receive:

- the same immutable investigation and claim identity;
- every material proposition;
- exactly the authoritative approved evidence IDs; and
- only `read_approved_evidence` and `build_position` permissions.

The defender constructs supporting and contextual mappings. The challenger
constructs contradictory and qualifying mappings and deterministic challenge
findings. LangGraph dispatches both role nodes concurrently and joins their
results only after both terminal records exist.

`search_calls` and `fetch_calls` are schema-constrained to zero. Any role
output referencing an evidence ID outside its assignment packet fails closed,
is persisted as a failed role result and triggers human review.

## Reconciliation and authority

The join does not vote, average prose or ask one agent to overrule another.
It calls the existing deterministic argument-ledger builder using:

- the approved evidence records;
- the typed verification packet;
- the provenance assessment; and
- the locked material propositions.

The resulting ordinary `ArgumentLedger` therefore retains all existing
reference validation and judgment-policy compatibility. The workflow records
whether it is exactly equivalent to the ledger generated by
`InvestigationService`. A missing or different authoritative ledger triggers
review rather than silently replacing authority.

The authoritative judge input now explicitly contains the approved argument
ledger and verification packet. Its evidence-ID invariant verifies that the
ledger's approved packet exactly matches the evidence packet.

## Durability and promoted-path integration

SQLite stores the workflow plan, each role result and the reconciled ledger.
Restart behavior is idempotent:

- a complete replay executes no role again;
- a simulated mid-fan-out restart reuses the completed defender result and
  executes only the unfinished challenger; and
- the final report is stable across replay.

The promoted durable graph state exposes the two role-result IDs and reconciled
ledger. Candidate research evidence is not admitted: the ledger must reference
exactly the authoritative `approved_evidence_ids`.

Direct orchestration remains the rollback path and creates no adversarial
workflow state.

- Complete and mid-fan-out restart recovery passed.
- The three-case promoted fixture pilot exposed two role results and an

No model call, live search, network fetch or PDF operation was used for the
Stage 8.7 argument subgraph.

## Deliberate limits

The role implementations are deterministic in the default development path.
The contracts permit bounded model use later, but model-generated positions
must pass the same approved-ID validation and deterministic reconciliation.
The subgraph reorganizes approved facts; it does not improve retrieval or
calibrate verdict confidence.
