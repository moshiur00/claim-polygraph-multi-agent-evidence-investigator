# ADR 0023: Promote bounded social-evidence governance

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 30 July 2026
- Phase: 10

## Context

Phase 10 separates social distribution from source authority and adds typed
account identity, authenticity evidence, attribution scope, post type,
original-source derivation, evidentiary-use eligibility, shared-origin
clustering, review routing, publication blocking, and transparent reporting.

The adversarial benchmark covers official announcements, eyewitness posts,
manipulated screenshots, repost cascades, deleted posts, and social links to
primary documents. Its machine gates passed, and Md Moshiur Rahman annotated
the targeted calibration packet with distinct approval from Md Rashedul Islam.

Stage 10.9 also re-executed the relevant recovery and security boundaries. The
audit found no regression in Phase 9 restart, checkpoint, paid-operation,
review, SSE, or direct-rollback guarantees.

## Proposed decision

Promote the Phase 10 social-evidence governance policy for the bounded local
Claim Polygraph NG deployment.

The unified authoritative LangGraph remains the default orchestrator.
`InvestigationService` operations remain the domain and persistence authority.
The direct sequential workflow remains the tested rollback.

Social material may be retained only within its typed permitted use.
Deterministic policy—not a model—controls eligibility, independence,
human-review routing, and publication safety.

## Scope

Included:

- local Docker and bounded single-host operation;
- public, permitted content and reliable archives;
- provisional reports before human judgment;
- mandatory publication blocking for unsafe social dependencies;
- human-reviewed final publication.

Excluded:

- private or restricted social-content collection;
- engagement or badges as truth signals;
- autonomous publication;
- authenticated multi-tenant or public-internet deployment;
- unbounded distributed workloads;
- calibrated factual-confidence claims.

## Consequences

Positive:

- social evidence is traceable without being over-promoted;
- cross-platform repetition cannot simulate independent corroboration;
- deleted, copied, or screenshot-only content receives conservative handling;
- original records are preferred and visibly linked;
- journalists can inspect why an item was used, limited, or excluded.

Costs and limitations:

- conservative routing can increase human-review volume;
- authenticity and original-source resolution may add latency;
- reviewer identity binding is not production authentication;
- live correctness still depends on retrieval quality and human judgment.

## Approval

Approved by Md Moshiur Rahman on 30 July 2026 through an explicit user
decision. Phase 10 is closed within the bounded scope defined above.
