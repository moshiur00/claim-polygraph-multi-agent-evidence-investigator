## CPNG-006

**Claim:** Germany was the world's third-largest economy by nominal GDP in
2023.

**Proposed interpretation:** Compare sovereign-country economies using
calendar-year 2023 nominal GDP converted to current U.S. dollars. Do not use
real GDP, purchasing-power parity, per-capita GDP, a forecast, or the European
Union as one economy.

**Proposed verdict:** `supported`

**Evidence:**

- [World Bank GDP API](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/DE;JP;US;CN/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD?date=2023&format=json&per_page=100)
- [Associated Press report](https://apnews.com/article/japan-economy-2023-gdp-893d53deba654c4924e4924f0b321cc5)
- Optional independent comparison:
  [IMF DataMapper](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/NGDPD%40WEO/USA/CHN/JPN/DEU/IND)

**Annotator checks:**

- [x] Re-run the World Bank query and record the access date and all four
  values: United States, China, Germany, and Japan.
- [x] Confirm the indicator is `NY.GDP.MKTP.CD` and that all observations are
  for 2023.
- [x] Check the same ordering in an independently produced IMF vintage and
  record whether it is an estimate or later revision.
- [x] Confirm AP's dollar-conversion qualification.
- [x] Decide whether revision sensitivity changes the label or only the
  rationale.
- [x] Annotator decision and rationale recorded.
- [x] Distinct approver independently confirmed the ranking.

**Completed decision:** The April 2026 IMF World Economic Outlook was selected
as the independent vintage. Its 2023 values were treated as revised historical
estimates. Revisions did not change Germany's third-place rank.

## CPNG-007

**Proposed interpretation:** “Founded” means creation or initial incorporation
of the venture that became Amazon. It does not mean the public launch of the
online store.

**Proposed verdict:** `supported`

**Evidence:**

- [Amazon 2016 Form 10-K on SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872417000011/amzn-20161231x10k.htm)
- [HistoryLink: Amazon—The Early Years](https://www.historylink.org/File/23230)

**Annotator checks:**

- [x] Verify the 10-K says incorporation occurred in Washington in 1994.
- [x] Verify the same filing separates incorporation from the July 1995
  website opening.
- [x] Verify HistoryLink's editorial account and its underlying references.
- [x] Decide whether ordinary readers understand “founded” as creation,
  incorporation, website launch, or another milestone.
- [x] Search for credible evidence supporting 1995 as a competing founding
  interpretation.
- [x] Annotator decision and rationale recorded.
- [x] Distinct approver independently approved the interpretation.

**Completed decision:** Ordinary “founded” was resolved as creation or
incorporation in 1994. The July 1995 commercial launch is a distinct event.

## CPNG-008

**Claim:** The European Union's General Data Protection Regulation began
applying on 25 May 2018.

**Proposed interpretation:** “Began applying” means the legal date of
application, not adoption, publication, or entry into force.

**Proposed verdict:** `supported`

**Evidence:**

- [Official GDPR HTML, Article 99](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng)
- [European Data Protection Board legal framework](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/legal-framework_en)

**Annotator checks:**

- [x] Read Article 99(1) and 99(2) together.
- [x] Confirm adoption on 27 April 2016, publication on 4 May 2016, entry into
  force on 24 May 2016, and application from 25 May 2018.
- [x] Confirm the EDPB page preserves this distinction.
- [x] Annotator decision and rationale recorded.
- [x] Distinct approver checked the controlling legal text.

**Completed decision:** Entry into force on 24 May 2016 was distinguished from
application beginning on 25 May 2018.

## CPNG-009

**Claim:** Cigarette smoking causes lung cancer.

**Proposed interpretation:** This is a population-level causal claim about
combustible-cigarette smoking. It does not say every smoker develops cancer,
that every lung cancer was caused by smoking, or that smoking is the only
cause.

**Proposed verdict:** `supported`

**Evidence:**

- [U.S. National Cancer Institute fact sheet](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/tobacco/cessation-fact-sheet)
- [World Health Organization lung-cancer fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/lung-cancer)

**Annotator checks:**

- [x] Locate NCI's direct causal wording.
- [x] Inspect the cited Surgeon General review chain supporting that wording.
- [x] Confirm WHO's statement and its discussion of other risk factors.
- [x] Verify that the rationale describes causation without necessary,
  sufficient, or individual-certainty overstatement.
- [x] Check whether a quantitative attributable-risk figure is needed for the
  rationale; it is not required merely to establish causation.
- [x] Annotator decision and rationale recorded.
- [x] Distinct approver independently checked both source families.

**Completed decision:** The population-level causal wording was approved
without implying that every smoker develops lung cancer or that smoking is the
only cause.

## CPNG-010

**Claim:** Taking vitamin C prevents the common cold.

**Proposed interpretation:** Read the unqualified claim as regular vitamin C
supplementation reducing cold incidence in the ordinary population.
“Prevents” does not mean shortening duration or reducing severity after a cold
occurs.

**Proposed verdict:** `misleading`

**Evidence:**

- [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD000980_vitamin-c-preventing-and-treating-common-cold)
- [NIH health-professional fact sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/)
- Optional recent context:
  [2023 severity meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10712193/)

**Annotator checks:**

- [x] Confirm the Cochrane review's ordinary-population incidence result.
- [x] Confirm the NIH page was updated in 2025 and preserves the
  general-population conclusion.
- [x] Record the dose and timing being judged.
- [x] Separate prevention from duration and severity.
- [x] Record the extreme-exercise, cold-exposure, and marginal-status
  qualifications.
- [x] Verify that the 2023 severity review concerns severity rather than
  general-population prevention.
- [x] Decide between `misleading` and `contradicted` under the agreed
  interpretation.
- [x] Annotator decision and rationale recorded.
- [x] Distinct approver independently checked the outcome distinctions.

**Completed decision:** `misleading` was selected because the broad claim
conceals limited subgroup support and confuses prevention with duration or
severity. `contradicted` was considered and rejected.
